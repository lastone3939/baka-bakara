# Image2 Prompt Pack

このゲームで使う画像は、最終的にImage2生成素材として `assets/` 配下へ保存する。
画像内に細かい日本語UI文字は入れず、文字はHTML側で重ねる。

## 共通スタイル

- 3D stylized premium mobile game art
- Japanese arcade casino energy, neon gold, electric blue, sharp red accents
- dramatic but readable, mobile portrait composition
- no watermark, no brand logo, no small text, no fake UI labels
- leave clear center/upper space for HTML title and buttons
- cinematic lighting, glossy chips, realistic cards, energetic particles

## Title Background

Save as: `assets/scenes/title-makesabi-v1.png`

```text
Primary request: Premium 3D mobile game title background for a surreal Japanese debt challenge game. A dark neon casino table blends with an airport runway and distant ocean, suggesting "win 100 million or swim home". Gold chips, baccarat cards, electric blue and red stage lights, cinematic sparks, expensive arcade-game atmosphere.
Composition: center area readable for large HTML title text, lower area has space for login/start button, no embedded readable text.
Avoid: real brand logos, copyrighted characters, tiny text, flat vector look, dull beige palette, overly dark unreadable background.
```
